Budget Autos Repair accepts rental requests from customers leaving a vehicle with the shop for repair. The current rental request page lists **$35 per day with up to 100 miles included per day**. A submitted request is not a confirmed reservation; availability and eligibility must be checked by the shop.

## Ask before arranging your drop-off

Tell the shop the planned repair date and when you expect to need transportation. A diagnostic appointment may reveal work that takes longer than expected, so discuss what happens if the repair schedule changes.

Do not assume a vehicle will be waiting just because you booked a repair. Ask for confirmation of both the repair appointment and rental arrangements before making plans around them.

## What information does the request need?

The [rental request form](/rental/) asks for contact and repair details, whether you have a valid driver’s license, and whether you have active New York auto insurance. It also requests photos of your license and insurance card so the shop can review the request.

Use the rental form for those documents, rather than uploading them with a repair estimate. Ask the shop about any eligibility question you cannot answer confidently.

## What should I confirm with the shop?

Before accepting a rental, confirm the total expected cost, what happens if you exceed the included mileage, any deposit or additional charges, insurance requirements, approved drivers, fuel expectations, and return arrangements. Those details should come from the actual rental terms.

Ask how an extension is handled if a part is delayed or the repair needs additional diagnosis. Do not assume another day is available without contacting the shop.

## Is this a loaner or an insurance-covered rental?

The site offers a paid rental request. It does not promise a complimentary loaner or that your insurer will pay. If you expect reimbursement, ask your insurer about the coverage that applies to your situation before relying on it.

[Request a rental](/rental/) or [call (607) 323-0236](tel:+16073230236) to discuss availability for your repair at 2344 Airport Rd, Johnson City. If you have not yet decided on the repair, you can first [send the estimate for a second opinion](/second-opinion/).
