**Only the axle that is worn.** Best is not the biggest ticket. If the fronts need brakes and the rears do not, we say that. Same idea on struts versus a simple alignment.

**A shop that will tell you no.** If the job is outside our lane, we say so up front. Taking a deposit and figuring it out later is not excellence.

**Warranty you can ask about.** Parts and labor coverage exists. Ask us for the terms that apply to your job. We will not hide them inside a range on a banner ad.
