The most useful starting point is **when the vibration happens**. Shaking at idle, at a particular road speed, and only under braking do not necessarily have the same cause.

If the vibration is sudden or severe, steering becomes difficult, or you suspect tire damage, slow down carefully and stop in a safe place. Arrange assistance rather than driving to reproduce it. [Bridgestone’s tire safety manual](https://www.bridgestonetire.com/content/dam/bridgestone/consumer/bst/warranties/BS_FS_PSR_MAINTENANCE%20AND%20SAFETY%20MANUAL_US_ENGLISH_JAN_2020.pdf) calls for professional evaluation of vibration, bulges, and irregular wear.

## Shaking at idle

If it happens while the vehicle is stationary, wheel balance is unlikely to explain that particular symptom. Engine running problems or mounts may need inspection. A flashing check-engine light with rough running calls for a safe stop and an engine shutdown. Read [the misfire guide](/blog/car-misfiring-can-i-drive/) before planning to drive in.

## Vibration at road speed

Tires, wheels, suspension, and driveline components are among the possibilities. Tell the technician where you feel it: through the steering wheel, seat, or whole vehicle. Also mention whether it started after a pothole, tire installation, rotation, or other repair.

Wheel balancing and wheel alignment address different things. Buying an alignment because “the car shakes” may leave the cause untouched. An inspection should establish what needs correcting.

## Shaking when braking

If vibration begins when you press the brake pedal, have the brakes and related components inspected. The shop may need to measure rotor condition and check calipers and mounting surfaces, rather than assuming a tire balance issue. See [brake noise and shaking under braking](/blog/signs-your-brakes-need-replacement-binghamton-ny/).

## What to tell the shop

Write down the conditions you have already noticed, approximate speed, warning lights, and recent work. Do not let go of the steering wheel, make abrupt maneuvers, or film while driving to create a demonstration.
